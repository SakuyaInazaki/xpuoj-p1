Production [`p1/kernel.py`](../../p1/kernel.py) was not modified, no P1 submission was made, and no candidate was promoted. A read-only platform check found P1 unchanged at **72.75 / best SID 141408**, submission count **2589**, latest SID **141474** terminal, and no `Pending`/`Running` submission. The current problem and language metadata expose no Triton or image version for `triton-dist`; it remains absent from advertised custom-test modes and `checkAvailability(requiredFlags=["custom-test"])` returns `available=false`, so neither a remote version nor distributed custom execution is publicly available.

Archived custom CID `96aa7f26-4e82-4b06-a159-303d97d40437` completed its deliberate diagnostic marker and validated all 540,672 outputs. In its single-GPU, register-resident, fixed-geometry legacy warp-MMA probe, INT4 reached **588.391 effective TOPS** versus FP8 **1019.145 effective TOPS**, or **57.7%** of FP8. These are workload-normalized effective rates for this probe, not absolute hardware peak throughput. Even with HBM, TMA, quantization, unpacking, scales, and end-to-end P1 work excluded, fixed INT4 was slower, so this direction is closed without parameter sweeps or production integration. Full artifacts are indexed in [results/INDEX.md](results/INDEX.md).

The first c4 output-TMA probe, CID `de45e5a9-16ed-475f-ab49-b733daa621d0`, combined `TensorDescriptor.store` with the production `flatten=True` loop and failed during candidate JIT/MLIR verification; it emitted no `P1MD`, so it says nothing about TMA-store correctness or speed. A zero-row-check-only successor was prepared but not submitted because it could not address that compiler failure. The one compatibility successor, CID `aa4ff079-1264-4b84-bff5-73398e324b8b`, kept the baseline at `flatten=True` and combined candidate output TMA with `flatten=False`. Full FP8, scale, final BF16, and numeric-zero checks all passed. Across three AB/BA/AB groups, DN+gather was **14.35–14.60% slower** and DN alone was **18.45–18.82% slower**. Baseline and candidate shared memory were both **229408 bytes**, disproving the estimate that output TMA must add 32 KiB of reported shared memory; registers rose **170→231**, but this result does not establish register count as the sole cause. This exact c4 output-TMA + no-flat combination is closed without parameter sweeps and is not generalized to other cases or TMA mechanisms.

The padded, branch-free successor kept both DN loops flattened and stored all tiles into padded C8. Its main comparison charged an independent GPU PAD_ROW build before DN and mapped gather. Initial CID `edea0c78-e34a-4bdb-b31d-82636ed70f0c` passed every exact math and mapping gate; DN-only was 2.80–3.18% faster and the taxed main path was 0.15–2.14% faster. Longer balanced confirmation CID `f5c14b6e-30cf-4e52-8c94-cf7bdb1a5595` reproduced all gates/resources and DN-only speedups in all six groups, but the main path split three faster and three slower; its ratio of means was **1.000875×**. The padded + independent PAD_ROW structure therefore does not provide a complete gain and is not promoted. The DN-body signal remains scoped evidence that output TMA can help this c4 geometry; TMA as a whole is not closed. Do not extend same-code timing or scan store parameters. Revisit only with a distinct, evidence-backed structure that removes the added preparation/indexing cost.
